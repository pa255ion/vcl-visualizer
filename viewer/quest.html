      How this works
      
    
        
  1. The headset app talks to 127.0.0.1:8093 on the headset, and
          adb forwards that port to this computer.
    2. 
        
  2. adb can run over the USB cable or over Wi-Fi. Connect headset switches it to Wi-Fi so you
          can unplug, and forwards the port.
    3. 
        
  3. Switching to Wi-Fi needs the cable once after each headset restart. Until then, Connect works
          without it, using the address above.
    4.
